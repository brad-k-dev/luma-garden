LUMA GARDEN / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Your garden progress stays on your device. Advertising and consent services may process information needed to provide their services.

1. Operator and scope

Brad Kim operates the Luma Garden app and this support website. For privacy questions, contact cloov2026@gmail.com.

2. Game data on your device

Puzzle sessions, completion records, stars, daily-garden records, flower placements and sound/haptic preferences are saved on your device so you can resume play. The app requires no account and does not upload these saves to a developer server or send gameplay records through a separate analytics SDK. There is no app-managed cloud sync. Operating-system backups depend on your Apple settings and policies.

3. Advertising and consent services

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for campaign interstitials and optional rewarded hints, and Google UMP for privacy choices. These services communicate with Google over the internet.

Google SDKs may process IP addresses (which can estimate general location), device/app identifiers, diagnostics, crash and performance information, and ad impressions and interactions. Purposes include ad delivery and measurement, fraud prevention and service improvement. Processing varies with consent, device and advertising settings.

When an AdMob privacy message is published, UMP may show an IDFA explainer followed by Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission request. Only if you allow tracking may the Google Mobile Ads SDK include the IDFA in ad requests. If you deny, ad requests can continue without the IDFA. Ad personalization and measurement depend on your choices and applicable regional settings. Test ads in development builds may also involve SDK network requests.

4. Consent and choices

The app checks consent through UMP at launch and presents a form when required. It requests ads only when the consent SDK permits it. When required, Advertising privacy settings in the app settings lets you change your choices. Your choices may affect ad availability or the ads served. Rewarded ads are optional; the core puzzles can be played without ad rewards.

5. Sharing and external links

Only when you choose to share, the app passes an image of your completed board, stars and rotation count to the iOS share sheet. The receiving app you select handles it under its own policy. Opening flower-source links connects to external websites governed by their privacy policies.

6. Support information, retention and deletion

When you email us, we use your address, message and attachments to respond and resolve the issue. Do not send passwords, verification codes or unnecessary sensitive information. We retain support information as needed to handle the request and delete it when no longer needed, subject to any legally required retention.

Deleting the app removes local game data; offloading the app may retain it. Data processed by Google or other providers is retained and deleted under their own policies, and is not necessarily deleted when you remove the app. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of support information or to ask about its processing.

7. External services and international processing

Google, which provides advertising and consent services, and email service providers may process information on servers outside your country. See the official resources below for their practices and safeguards.

8. Children’s privacy

The app has no registration form collecting names or birth dates. Advertising SDK processing is described above. Parents or guardians can contact us about a child’s information or request deletion using the email below.

9. This website

This website has no ads, analytics scripts, external web fonts or input forms. Language selection uses page links without cookies or local storage. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process access information such as IP addresses for security and service delivery.

10. Changes

We update this page and its effective date when features or information practices change.
